# LG-011 — Game over and rematch

Phase 2 · Table
Depends on: LG-010
Blocks: LG-012, LG-015

## Outcome

When the rules match is terminal, input stops and the screen names the player who lost. Rematch deals a new classic match with the same players and rotates who starts (P4). A second control clears back to a bare table only if home does not exist yet; LG-012 replaces that control with Home.

## Acceptance

- No tap on the board grows the line after the result is shown.
- Two rematches in a row give the start to two different players, in seat order.
- The new deal uses a new seed.
- The loss belongs to the player who could not move, matching the rules result.

## On the iPhone

- Play a 2-player game through to the loss screen, then rematch.
- After the result, board taps do not grow the line.

## Out of scope

- Stats, names editor, share sheet.
