# LG-004 — Legal growth

Phase 1 · Classic rules
Depends on: LG-003
Blocks: LG-005, LG-008

## Outcome

From a match, the package lists every legal growth of the head and every legal growth of the tail.

A growth is legal when the target is in bounds, is not a wall, is not already on the line, and is a king-move away from that end (orthogonal or diagonal). A diagonal is illegal when the opposite diagonal between the two side dots is already a segment. Orthogonal steps are not blocked by that test. Dots outside `0 .. 7` never appear. The same empty dot may appear twice, once per end, when both ends can reach it.

## Acceptance

- Opening position on an empty neighborhood lists up to 8 distinct targets, each tagged for both ends because head and tail are the same dot. Callers can still treat it as one set of targets.
- A constructed opposite diagonal is absent from the list. Occupying both side dots without a segment between them does not remove the diagonal.
- A wall neighbor is absent. A dot already on the line is absent.
- Tests build positions directly rather than only through random deals, so the crossing case is stable.

## Shape

[port-patterns.md](../port-patterns.md). Neighbors are bounds-checked index math. A diagonal block is one segment-key lookup. Moves are `{end, cell}`, and a cell beside both ends is two moves.

## Out of scope

- Applying the move, UI highlighting, the prototype's head-first click priority (that is LG-006).
