# LG-015 — Art direction

Phase 5 · Feel
Depends on: LG-011
Blocks: LG-016

## Outcome

A short direction note at `idea/art-direction.md` that LG-016 can implement. It picks the modern hot-seat look: palette, dot and end shapes, ink character, wall character, type, and the timings for draw, target pulse, turn pass, and loss.

The prototype notebook and stain set are reference for the rules, not the shipping skin. They may be named as a later "paper" theme. This note describes the default theme only.

## Acceptance

- The note specifies tokens (color, stroke, duration, easing) rather than a mood paragraph alone.
- It states how head and tail differ, and how a legal dot differs from a quiet dot and from a wall.
- It states a turn should read in about a second, and that 8×8 must stay tappable with a thumb.

## Out of scope

- Final asset files, sound production, a second theme.
