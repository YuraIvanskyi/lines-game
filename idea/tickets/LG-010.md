# LG-010 — Tap to grow

Phase 2 · Table
Depends on: LG-006, LG-009
Blocks: LG-011

## Outcome

Tapping a legal dot grows the line. A dot only one end can reach plays immediately. A dot both ends can reach waits until the player indicates the end, then plays. Tapping empty space, a wall, or an occupied quiet dot does nothing. After a growth, the next player's targets are shown.

## Acceptance

- A full turn updates the match through the rules package, not through widget-side geometry checks.
- The ambiguous-dot path can commit either end, and cancelling the choice leaves the match unchanged.
- Illegal taps do not change the player to move.
- Widget or integration coverage hits a unique-end tap, an ambiguous tap, and a cancelled choice.

## Shape

[port-patterns.md](../port-patterns.md). A tap hits the cached cell centers, then submits a move to `apply`. The widget does not re-check king-moves or crossings itself.

## Out of scope

- Animation polish, haptics, the pass-the-phone copy (LG-013).
