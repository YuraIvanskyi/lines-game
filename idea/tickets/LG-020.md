# LG-020 — Second skin, no checkout

Phase 8 · Cosmetics spike
Depends on: LG-016
Blocks: —

## Outcome

Settings can switch between the default theme and one alternate theme. The choice persists locally and restyles the board through the existing tokens. No price, no restore-purchases, no network.

The ticket also adds `idea/monetization.md`: what could be sold later (board, ink, dot, flourish sets), and the line that stays free (classic rules, offline play, a complete default skin). Billing SDKs are named only as a follow-up, not integrated.

## Acceptance

- Both themes are usable for a full game and the choice survives restart.
- `packages/rules` does not import either theme.
- `idea/monetization.md` states the free line and the cosmetic surface in a page or less.

## Out of scope

- Real charges, ads, limited-time energy, mode packs that change rules.
