# LG-003 — Seeded classic deal

Phase 1 · Classic rules
Depends on: LG-001
Blocks: LG-004

## Outcome

`packages/rules` can deal a classic match: an 8×8 grid, two or more players in order, one anchor that is both head and tail, and walls on other dots.

The deal function takes an integer seed. The same seed and player count always produce the same anchor and the same walls. Wall probability is 0.05. The anchor is never a wall. Players are an ordered list of ids and display names supplied by the caller. The package does not invent Shuri, Steve, Chen, or Pylyp.

## Acceptance

- Two deals with the same seed and players compare equal.
- Two deals with different seeds are allowed to differ.
- A dealt anchor is connected, is both ends, and is not a wall.
- Grid coordinates are `x` rightward and `y` downward, in `0 .. 7`.
- Unit tests cover the equal-seed case, the anchor guarantees, and a player count of 2 and of 4.

## Shape

[port-patterns.md](../port-patterns.md). A deal is a match value: cell indexes, a wall bit grid, head and tail on the anchor index. No per-cell objects, no image loads, no shared `random` stream with visuals.

## Out of scope

- Legal moves, drawing, persistence.
