# LG-016 — Motion, sound, tokens

Phase 5 · Feel
Depends on: LG-013, LG-014, LG-015
Blocks: LG-017, LG-020

## Outcome

The table follows `idea/art-direction.md`. Segment draw, target pulse, end markers, turn pass, and game over animate. A small set of sounds and haptics marks a committed growth, an illegal tap, a turn pass, and a loss. The board reads colors, strokes, and durations from the default theme tokens.

Play stays offline. Missing audio files degrade to silence rather than crashing.

## Acceptance

- A growth plays its ink animation once, then the new targets appear.
- Illegal taps do not play the success haptic.
- Screenshot of head, tail, target, wall, and quiet dot matches the direction note's distinctions.
- No color literal for ink or dots remains in the board widget; they come from the theme.

## On the iPhone

- A committed growth is audible and haptic. An illegal tap does not use that haptic.
- Speaker volume off still leaves the game playable.
- A turn still reads in about a second.

## Out of scope

- Music bed, voice, store listing video, Android-specific audio routing.
