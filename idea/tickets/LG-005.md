# LG-005 — Turns and loss

Phase 1 · Classic rules
Depends on: LG-004
Blocks: LG-006, LG-007, LG-008

## Outcome

Applying a legal growth marks both dots connected, appends one segment owned by the player to move, moves that end, and advances the turn. Applying an illegal growth leaves the match unchanged and reports rejection.

The opening growth leaves the anchor as the tail and the new dot as the head.

The player to move loses when their legal list is empty. The match then exposes a terminal result naming that player. Further growth is rejected.

## Acceptance

- After the first growth, head and tail are different dots and the anchor is the tail.
- A rejected move does not advance the player and does not add a segment.
- A scripted fill that exhausts the board ends on the player who cannot move, with a stable result value.
- Tests cover opening split, rejection, and a terminal position.

## Shape

[port-patterns.md](../port-patterns.md). `apply` returns a new match and leaves the previous one intact. Players are a list plus a turn index, not a cycle iterator.

## Out of scope

- Choosing between two ends when both are legal (LG-006). The apply function takes an explicit end.
- Rematch rotation (the table does that in LG-011).
