# LG-002 — Portrait phone frame

Phase 0 · Phone shell
Depends on: LG-001
Blocks: LG-009

## Outcome

The iOS build is locked to portrait. The placeholder respects the safe area, including the home indicator. Layout is aimed at a modern phone, not a tablet-only canvas.

## Acceptance

- Orientation is locked to portrait in the iOS project.
- Content is inset for the safe area in the layout code.

## On the iPhone

Install the LG-001 artifact with Sideloadly, as in [ios-device.md](../ios-device.md).

- The placeholder opens in portrait on the iPhone 16 Pro.
- Nothing sits under the Dynamic Island or the home indicator.
- This is the first proof that the Actions artifact, Sideloadly, and Developer Mode work together.

## Out of scope

- Tablet layout, foldables, Android-specific insets.
