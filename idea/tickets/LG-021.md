# LG-021 — Online contract note

Phase 9 · Online note
Depends on: LG-007
Blocks: —

## Outcome

`idea/online.md` states the contract for a later remote opponent. It does not add sockets, accounts, or a vendor.

The contract: a match is mode id, seed, players, and the move log. A client proposes one growth. `packages/rules` accepts or rejects it. The canonical log is the match. Hot-seat is the reference writer of that log. The note lists open choices (who deals the seed, how a dropped player loses, what a client may cache) and does not pretend they are decided.

## Acceptance

- The note matches the JSON record from LG-007.
- It names hot-seat as the implementation that already satisfies the log half of the contract.
- It contains no SDK choice presented as settled.

## Out of scope

- Servers, matchmaking, chat, anti-cheat beyond "replay the log".
