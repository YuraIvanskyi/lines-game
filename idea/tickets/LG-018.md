# LG-018 — Resume a match

Phase 7 · On-device memory
Depends on: LG-012
Blocks: LG-019

## Outcome

Killing and reopening the app during a classic game restores that match from the local move log, or offers a clear discard. A finished match is not restored as if it were in progress. All of this is on device.

## Acceptance

- After a process restart mid-game, the board shows the same ends, segments, walls, and player to move.
- Discard returns to home with no live match.
- A finished game reopens on home or on the result, and does not accept more growth.

## On the iPhone

- Force-quit mid-game, reopen, and the same ends, segments, walls, and player to move are back.
- Discard, reopen, and home has no live match.

## Out of scope

- iCloud, accounts, several simultaneous matches.
