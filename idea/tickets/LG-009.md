# LG-009 — Draw the match

Phase 2 · Table
Depends on: LG-002, LG-008
Blocks: LG-010

## Outcome

A portrait screen paints one classic match on the 8×8 lattice: the line, distinct head and tail (P5), stable segment ink (P6), walls, and the dots that are legal for the player to move. The player to move is named. Nothing is tappable yet.

Placeholder geometry is acceptable. Final motion is LG-016. Colors come from a single token object even if only one theme exists.

## Acceptance

- An 8×8 deal lays out without a scroll view in the board widget.

## On the iPhone

- On the iPhone 16 Pro (402×874 pt portrait), the full 8×8 grid sits below the Dynamic Island and above the home indicator.
- A thumb can land on one dot without hitting its neighbor. If it cannot, the layout changes before LG-010.
- Head and tail are distinguishable in a screenshot, including on the opening anchor.
- Extending the line in a widget test or a debug rebuild does not recolor older segments.
- Walls read as blocked, not as tappable dots.
- Legal targets read as targets. Other empty dots stay quiet.

## Shape

[port-patterns.md](../port-patterns.md). The widget paints a match snapshot. Hit centers and committed ink paths are cached. There is no per-frame scene loop and no port of `Node.draw`.

## Out of scope

- Gestures, game over layout, home.
