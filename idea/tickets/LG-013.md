# LG-013 — How-to and pass the phone

Phase 3 · Pickup
Depends on: LG-012
Blocks: LG-016

## Outcome

A first-launch how-to is one screen: the line grows from either highlighted end, tap a marked dot, the player who cannot grow loses. It can be skipped and reopened from home. It is not a modal on every match.

Between turns the table shows whose turn it is and a pass-the-phone cue strong enough to read at arm's length. The cue does not require an extra confirmation tap before the next player can move.

## Acceptance

- The how-to is reachable from home after it has been skipped.
- A turn change updates the name and the cue without leaving the board.
- The next player can tap a target immediately.

## On the iPhone

- Hand the phone over. The turn cue is readable at arm's length.
- The next player can tap a target with no confirm step in between.

## Out of scope

- Animated tutorial, localization beyond English, voiceover.
