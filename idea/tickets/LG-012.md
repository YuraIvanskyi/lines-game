# LG-012 — Home and table setup

Phase 3 · Pickup
Depends on: LG-011
Blocks: LG-013, LG-014

## Outcome

Cold launch shows a home screen with one primary Play action. Play starts a 2-player classic game with default names. A secondary path sets the count to 3 or 4 and edits names, then starts. From game over, Home returns here and Rematch stays on the table.

Default names are dull and local (`Player 1`, `Player 2`), not the prototype cast.

## Acceptance

- From a fresh install, Play is the first action and the second tap is the first move on the board. Count and names are not on that path.
- Choosing 4 players deals four seats and cycles through all four before repeating.
- Empty names are rejected or replaced with the default for that seat.
- Home from game over does not resume the finished match.

## On the iPhone

- From a cold launch, Play and then the first move are two taps.
- A 4-player setup deals four seats.

## Out of scope

- Persistence across process death (LG-018), avatars, accounts.
