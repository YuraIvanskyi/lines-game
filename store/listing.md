Draft copy for App Store Connect and Play Console. Limits are noted next to each field. The grid size (6×6) is left out on purpose: future modes will use other sizes.

If "ENDS" is already taken as a store name, keep "ENDS" first and add a plain descriptor after it (e.g. "ENDS — line game"). The home-screen label stays "ENDS" either way.

## English

**Name** (≤30): ENDS

**App Store subtitle** (≤30): Grow the line. Don't get stuck.

**Play short description** (≤80): Pass-the-phone line duel: grow the line from either end, don't get stuck.

**App Store keywords** (≤100, comma-separated, no spaces needed):
line,dots,board,duel,hot seat,pass and play,2 player,offline,paper game,strategy

**App Store promotional text** (≤170): One line, two ends, no way out. A pocket version of the paper game we played at school, for two to four players on one phone, or against the computer.

**Description** (≤4000):

ENDS is a small strategy game for friends sitting next to each other.

There is one line on the board, and it has two ends: the ring and the square. On your turn, grow the line by one dot from either end. The line can't cross itself, can't run through walls, and can't slip between two dots that are already joined diagonally. Sooner or later someone runs out of moves, and that player loses.

• Pass and play: two, three or four players on one phone
• Play the computer on Easy or Hard
• Fully offline, no account, no ads, no data collected
• Quick rounds and an instant rematch
• English and Ukrainian

Started as a pencil-and-paper game between two school friends. Now it fits in your pocket.

**What's new** (1.0): First release.
